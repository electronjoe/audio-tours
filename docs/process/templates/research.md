## How to fill this in

Gather at least three times what the tour can use, across every category, for the region and
for each candidate stop. One line per item:

`- <fact> — <publisher, URL, date> — confirmed | single source | disputed — hook: <why a child or an adult would care>`

`confirmed` means two independent sources or one authoritative source. Only confirmed facts
become key claims in a script. Follow Wikipedia's citations rather than citing it.
