Balance:

- [ ] No two anchor stories in a row from the same category.
- [ ] At least 2 stops with wildlife likely on that stretch, and one with something to spot or
      count.
- [ ] At least 2 stops connect to the national or state picture, with the main sides given
      fairly.
- [ ] 2–4 songs, not on back-to-back stops or before a short gap.
- [ ] 1–3 guest moments, ideally an expert, a historical figure and an animal.
- [ ] 2–4 SFX per stop where they add something.
- [ ] First Nations history is present and handled with care.
- [ ] The day has a shape: gripping first, the big story at the parked stop, a lighter close.
