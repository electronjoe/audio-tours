# Hobart to Russell Falls: stop 6 interview

The platypus moment for stop 6, in the task 005 script format: the narrator interviews a
platypus, over a river bed, with a splash. It is a separate draft, built into
`build/stop-6-platypus/`, so the published stop 6 is untouched. Render and master it with
`--draft stop-6-platypus.md`. Facts come from the stop 6 research notes (Australian Museum,
AMNH, Australian Platypus Conservancy, regional visitor information).

## Cast

- PLATYPUS → voices.platypus: a cheerful, quick, slightly cheeky young platypus. A light
  Australian accent, not a caricature. Clearly different from the narrator: younger, faster,
  higher.

## Script: Stop 6 — The Hunter with an Electric Face

[SFX — river, under]

If a platypus could talk, it might say something like this. Excuse me, platypus? Have you got a minute?

PLATYPUS: A minute? I'm on my dinner break. Make it quick.

When you dive, what do you do with your eyes?

PLATYPUS: Shut them. My ears too. And my nostrils.

[SFX — splash]

Then how do you find dinner?

PLATYPUS: With my bill. Living things give off tiny electric signals, even a shrimp under a stone. My bill picks them up.

Do you zap them?

PLATYPUS: No zapping! I just listen. It's like a secret message that says: dinner, this way.

And is it true you hatched from an egg?

PLATYPUS: True! I've got fur, my mum fed me milk, and I still started out as an egg.

[SFX — river, stop]

Thank you, platypus. Passengers: if you were a river explorer, which sense would you switch off first?

[PAUSE — 3 SECONDS]

## Audio notes

- `river`: bed, loop. Gentle shallow river over stones in quiet forest, ducked under speech.
- `splash`: one-shot. A small animal slipping into calm water, in the gap after "nostrils".
